# Rosabon Customer Intelligence Operating System (VoC PulseIQ)
## How to Use Option A, Option B, and Option C

**Target Role:** Customer Intelligence Analyst (Junior)  
**Organization:** Rosabon Financial Services  
**Active URL:** `http://127.0.0.1:5173/`

---

### Quick Access Guide

#### ⚔️ Option A: Competitor & Market Intelligence Radar
* **How to open:** In the top navigation, click **`2. Customer Voices`** $\rightarrow$ select subtab **`⚔️ Competitor Radar (Opt. A)`**.
* **What you do:**
  1. Review the **Turnaround Gap** (+6 to 9 days slower on SME loans vs. commercial banks).
  2. Click on any competitor in the **Head-to-Head Matrix** (*Access Bank*, *Renmoney*, *Carbon*) to see their specific APRs, collateral rules, and Rosabon counter-strategies.
  3. Check the **VoC Competitor Citation Heatmap** to see how many customers mentioned rival lenders.
* **Why it matters:** Fulfills your mandate to monitor competitor developments and protect Rosabon clients from defection.

---

#### 🧠 Option B: Psychographic & Behavioral Borrower Personas
* **How to open:** In the top navigation, click **`2. Customer Voices`** $\rightarrow$ select subtab **`🧠 Borrower Personas (Opt. B)`**.
* **What you do:**
  1. Click through the 4 borrower archetypes:
     * **The Ambitious SME Builder** (₦15M–₦85M working capital; values approval speed).
     * **The Salary-Backed Pragmatist** (₦200k–₦2.5M salary advance; values zero hidden fees).
     * **The Conservative Wealth Preserver** (₦25M–₦150M REAP investor; values human RM trust).
     * **The Asset & Fleet Optimizer** (₦15M–₦150M lease client; values vehicle maintenance uptime).
  2. Use the **4 Psychographic Dimensions** (*Motivations, Decision Logic, Churn Triggers, Retention Strategy*) when drafting briefs for Marketing and Product teams.
* **Why it matters:** Fulfills your mandate for behavioral and psychographic customer segmentation.

---

#### 🖥️ Option C: Executive Boardroom Presentation Deck
* **How to open:** In the top navigation, click **`3. Executive Reports`** $\rightarrow$ click the blue button **`🖥️ Present Boardroom Deck (Opt. C)`** in the top right.
* **How to present:**
  * **Next Slide:** Press **`→` (Right Arrow)** or **Spacebar**.
  * **Previous Slide:** Press **`←` (Left Arrow)**.
  * **Exit:** Press **`Esc`** or click **`Exit Presentation`**.
* **What you present:**
  * Slide 1: Executive Title block with Rosabon branding.
  * Slide 2: Macro VoC Health (NPS +38, CSAT 4.1/5.0, ₦185M Churn Flight Risk).
  * Slide 3: Cross-functional operational hotspots (Sales, CX, Product, Transformation).
  * Slide 4: Competitor Benchmarking Radar (Rosabon vs Banks vs FinTechs).
  * Slide 5: Strategic ROI Recommendations for Executive Sign-Off (achieving $\ge 40\%$ adoption).
* **Why it matters:** Eliminates the end-of-month panic of building PowerPoint decks; you can present directly from the app in C-Suite meetings.
