## Option A: Competitor & Market Intelligence Radar

### 📍 Where to Find It
1. Click **`2. Customer Voices`** in the top navigation.
2. In the sub-menu, click **`⚔️ Competitor Radar (Opt. A)`**.

### 🎯 Purpose & Business Value
Your job description tasks you with monitoring *"market developments, competitor activities, and evolving customer expectations"*. In the Nigerian financial services space, Rosabon competes against:
1. **Tier-1 Commercial Banks** (*Access Bank, GTBank, Zenith*): Aggressive on interest rates and 72-hour pre-approved business lines.
2. **Direct NBFI Rivals** (*Renmoney, Page Financials*): Dominating fast retail salary loans.
3. **Pure FinTechs** (*Carbon, FairMoney*): Ultra-fast app disbursement under 10 minutes with transparent fees.

### 🛠️ How to Use It Day-to-Day

#### Step 1: Analyze the Speed & Pricing Variance
* Review the **Summary Metric Cards**:
  * **SME Turnaround Gap:** Displays the +6 to 9 days variance between Rosabon’s 9–12 day credit committee SLA vs. Commercial Banks' 72-hour pre-approved lines.
  * **APR Pricing Differential:** Highlights where banks undercut on corporate interest rates (+1.5% to 3.0%).
  * **Rosabon Core Moat:** Highlights Rosabon’s structural advantage: **Flexible Collateral Policy** (accepting asset leases, equipment debentures, and invoice discounting where banks reject titles).

#### Step 2: Use the Head-to-Head Comparison Matrix
* Click on any competitor row in the table (*Commercial Banks*, *Consumer NBFIs*, or *Digital FinTechs*).
* The **Selected Competitor Deep Dive** panel below will automatically refresh:
  * **Why Borrowers Threaten Defection:** Read the exact triggers driving clients to rival lenders.
  * **How Rosabon Wins the Deal:** Identifies counter-arguments your Relationship Managers (RMs) can use in client negotiations.
  * **Analyst Recommendation:** Gives you concrete policy inputs for the Head of Credit and Head of Product.

#### Step 3: Monitor VoC Competitor Citations
* Look at the **VoC Competitor Citation Heatmap** on the right to see how many times borrowers mentioned *Access Bank*, *Carbon*, or *Renmoney* in actual tickets and meeting debriefs.
* Click **`View Filtered Verbatims →`** to read the exact customer quotes.

## Option B: Psychographic & Behavioral Borrower Personas Studio

### 📍 Where to Find It
1. Click **`2. Customer Voices`** in the top navigation.
2. In the sub-menu, click **`🧠 Borrower Personas (Opt. B)`**.

### 🎯 Purpose & Business Value
Fulfills Key Responsibility B: *"Support psychographic and behavioral segmentation initiatives aimed at understanding customer motivations, preferences, and financial decision-making patterns."*

Instead of treating all borrowers as generic data rows, this studio breaks Rosabon's customer base into **4 core financial archetypes**.

### 👥 The 4 Rosabon Financial Archetypes

#### 1. The Ambitious SME Builder
* **Profile:** Commercial Entrepreneur, Importer, Logistics Contractor (₦15M – ₦85M exposure).
* **Products:** *Rosabon SME Working Capital Loan*, *Invoice Discounting*.
* **Psychological Motivation:** **Speed over Interest Rate.** Needs inventory turnover before seasonal supply windows close.
* **Friction Trigger:** Sequential appraisal delays and legal document transit.
* **Actionable Retention Strategy:** Create an Express Underwriting Lane with a 48h SLA for repeat borrowers with verified records.

#### 2. The Salary-Backed Pragmatist
* **Profile:** Employed Corporate Professional (₦200k – ₦2.5M exposure).
* **Products:** *Rosabon Quick Cash*, *Rosabon Personal Loan (RPL)*.
* **Psychological Motivation:** **Predictability & No Surprises.** Borrowing for emergency medical, school fees, or rent gaps.
* **Friction Trigger:** Unexpected administrative processing fees; auto-debit deductions firing at 2:00 AM before salary clears.
* **Actionable Retention Strategy:** All-In Repayment Calculator showing exact net deductions; automated 48-hour Remita grace period.

#### 3. The Conservative Wealth Preserver
* **Profile:** Senior Executive, Retiree, High-Net-Worth Individual (₦25M – ₦150M+ placement).
* **Products:** *Rosabon Earning Plan (REAP)*, *Treasury Notes*.
* **Psychological Motivation:** **Trust & Capital Preservation.** Demands dedicated human touch and audited maturity certificates.
* **Friction Trigger:** Portal display glitches on rollover certificates; forced self-serve migration without white-glove support.
* **Actionable Retention Strategy:** Hybrid "White-Glove Assisted Migration" desk at Victoria Island and Abuja branches.

#### 4. The Asset & Fleet Optimizer
* **Profile:** Logistics Director, Fleet Manager (₦15M – ₦150M+ exposure).
* **Products:** *Rosabon Consumer Asset Lease*, *Commercial Fleet Lease*.
* **Psychological Motivation:** **Uptime & Operational Continuity.** Values maintenance inspection support and rapid insurance replacement.
* **Friction Trigger:** Wrongful recovery notices to guarantors when payment gateways lag batch settlement.
* **Actionable Retention Strategy:** Direct payment webhook listener to prevent premature recovery alerts.
