# Rosabon Financial Services — Voice of Customer (VoC) Intelligence OS
**Role:** Customer Intelligence Analyst (Junior) • *Succession-Track Intelligence Engine*

---

## Executive Overview
**VoC PulseIQ** is an all-in-one Customer Intelligence Operating System built specifically for **Rosabon Financial Services** to systematically capture, organize, analyze, and translate qualitative customer feedback, credit appraisal friction, and cross-channel sentiment into executive-ready business insights.

The platform bridges the gap across Rosabon's **7 operational departments**:
1. **Sales & Relationship Managers (RMs)** (Corporate RMs, SME Account Officers, Auto-Dealer Partners)
2. **Customer Experience (CX) & Operations** (Contact center, WhatsApp desk, issue resolution)
3. **Product & Digital Growth** (Rosabon Mobile App, Quick Cash, Open Banking onboarding)
4. **Credit Appraisal & Recovery Ops** (Underwriting turnaround, Remita direct debit, restructuring)
5. **Marketing & Communications** (Lead campaigns, Google Play & App Store reviews)
6. **Digital Transformation** (Branch-to-app migration, biometric verification, digital certificates)
7. **Executive Management** (C-Suite & Managing Director Strategic Briefings)

## Core Application Workspaces

### 1. Executive Pulse & CX Metrics
* Real-time calculation of **NPS** (+38), **CSAT** (4.1/5.0), and **CES** (3.1/7.0).
* Portfolio Sentiment breakdown (Positive, Neutral, Negative) across Rosabon credit and investment lines.
* Immediate **Critical Churn Alerts** for high-exposure borrowers.

### 2. Multi-Source Ingestion & Hygiene Hub
* **Universal CSV/Excel Drag-and-Drop**: Auto-maps varied column headers from Zendesk, Google Forms, and CRM.
* **Quick RM / Loan Officer Debrief Logger**: Rapid entry form for 1-on-1 client visits and credit rejection notes.
* **Data Integrity Auditor**: Live verification of 99.4% data cleanliness for executive audit compliance.

### 3. Qualitative Intelligence & Thematic Explorer
* Dynamic filtering by **Department**, **Rosabon Product Line**, **Sentiment**, and **Churn Risk**.
* Verbatim inspection drawer showing customer quotes, root causes, and cross-functional hand-offs.
* Highlighting core products: *Rosabon Personal Loan (RPL), Consumer Asset Lease, SME Working Capital, Fleet Lease, REAP Treasury, and Quick Cash*.

### 4. 48-Hour SLA Request Copilot
* Live countdown timers on active stakeholder requests.
* "Ask VoC Anything" natural language prompt synthesizer generating 1-page executive memos.
* 1-Click Copy-to-Clipboard & Print/PDF export.

### 5. Monthly Executive Briefing Builder
* C-Suite & Board presentation document covering Macro VoC Health, Departmental Synthesis, Top 3 Emerging Risks, and Quantified ROI Recommendations.
* 100% compliant with monthly executive briefing schedules.

### 6. Closed-Loop Adoption & Retention Tracker
* Interactive Kanban pipeline tracking recommendations from *Proposed* to *Implemented*.
* Quantified financial impact (e.g. "+₦18M monthly disbursements", "Protects ₦185M SME portfolio").
